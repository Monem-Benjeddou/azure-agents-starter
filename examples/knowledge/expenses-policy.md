# Travel and Expenses Policy

Employees may book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for
flights of 6 hours or more, with manager approval recorded before booking.

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night in capital cities and 130 EUR elsewhere.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip, with itemised receipts for any
expense above 25 EUR. Late reports require approval from the finance team.
